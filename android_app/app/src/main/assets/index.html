Historical Timeline of the Lineage

c. 1046 BC

Mythological Origins & State of Zhu (祝国)

Descendant of the Yellow Emperor's Minister of Fire (Zhurong 祝融) is enfeoffed by King Wu of Zhou in modern-day Shandong. Nobles adopt "Zhu" (祝) as their permanent surname.

Han – Song Dynasties

Taiyuan Commandery (太原郡) Seat

The clan establishes its renowned seat in Taiyuan (Shanxi), producing scholars and magistrates, before migrating southward to Fujian (Putian / Dexing).

Early Ming (1300s–1400s)

Ocean Crossing to Hainan

Lord Zhu Dehui (德辉公) leaves Fujian upon appointment as imperial official in Wanyang (Wanning / 万州), establishing the mother branch in Hainan.

Mid-Ming (c. 15th Century)

Founding of Wenchang Branch (Gaolong Village)

5th-generation descendant Lord Zhu Gao (祝高) settles in Gaolong Village, Wenchang (高隆村), becoming the 1st Founding Patriarch of the Wenchang lineage.

1895 (Guangxu 21st Year)

The Sacred Cross-County Pact

14th-Gen scholar Zhu Xinchuan (祝心传) authors the Joint Lineage Preface, mandating that Wanning and Wenchang must exchange genealogy copies whenever updated.

Late 19th – 20th Century

Xia Nanyang Diaspora (下南洋)

Clan members emigrate across Southeast Asia to Singapore, Malaysia, and Vietnam, opening trading outposts and establishing thriving overseas families.

1927 – 1949

Revolutionary Martyrs

Eight Zhu clan members from Gaolong village sacrifice their lives during the national wars; commemorated in Guangdong provincial annals.

2003 – 2007

The Great Lineage Restoration

16th-Gen elder Zhu Zhaoke (祝朝克) spends his retirement traveling across 20+ villages, locates the 1895 pact, and compiles the definitive 96-page Lineage Book.

Present Era

The 20-Year Mandate

The 19th ("Ming" / 明) and 20th ("Yun" / 运) generations carry the generational responsibility for preserving and updating family records.

Key Figures of the Clan

Lord Zhu Gao (祝高)

1st Generation

Founding Patriarch of Wenchang. Migrated from Wanzhou to Gaolong Village, establishing the family seat that has endured for over 500 years and 21 generations.

Zhu Xinchuan (祝心传)

14th Generation

Distinguished scholar who authored the 1895 Joint Lineage Preface (祝氏联谱序), formalizing the cross-county pact uniting Wanning and Wenchang.

Zhu Zhaoke (祝朝克)

16th Generation

The author and compiler of the 2007 Lineage. Spent 5 years in retirement traveling to 20+ villages, recovering grave inscriptions and reuniting fragmented branches.

Zhu Shengjin (祝圣进)

18th Generation

Key patriarchal elder directly preceding your generation in the Gaolong Village paternal lineage.

Zhu Mingcan (祝明灿)

19th Generation

Recorded on pages 44–45 alongside generational brothers (Mingwei, Mingcheng, Mingjian, Mingtai), representing the contemporary generation carrying the family legacy.

Gaolong Village Martyrs

20th Century

Eight brave clan members who sacrificed their lives during wartime resistance; permanently enshrined in the Guangdong Revolutionary Martyrs Directory.
